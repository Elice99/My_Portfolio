THE PROBLEM

Data quality issues can silently damage downstream decisions. TrustLake scores datasets on a deterministic Trust Score (0-100) and explains why in plain English, with a strict boundary: the AI explains and recommends, deterministic rules enforce, humans approve.

APPROACH

Currently at Stage 1 of a 10-stage roadmap -- landing page shipped, moving into backend foundation (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy) with a provider-agnostic AI layer that works with AI fully disabled.

OUTCOME

Pre-MVP. Full PRD, principles doc, and staged build roadmap are already written -- this case study will fill in with real metrics as each stage ships.
