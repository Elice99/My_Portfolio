THE PROBLEM

Hosts need to price competitively against neighbourhood benchmarks in New York City's short-term rental market but usually have no structured way to see where they sit relative to comparable listings.

APPROACH

I built an ETL pipeline into PostgreSQL, wrote 16 SQL analytics queries, and trained an XGBoost price prediction model served through a FastAPI /predict endpoint, with a Streamlit app for exploring the market data.

OUTCOME

RMSE $83.10, R² 0.4553 on price prediction -- a documented baseline for how much listing attributes explain price variation, not a claim of fully accurate market pricing.
