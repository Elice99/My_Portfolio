THE PROBLEM

GlowMart Nigeria's Sales Manager said Port Harcourt and Kano were short of stock. The Inventory Manager said stock was already shared equally across the five cities. The Managing Director wanted evidence, not opinions.

APPROACH

I built a three-page Excel dashboard on 15,000 transactions from 2022 to 2024. Power Query cleaned the data, Power Pivot held the model, and PivotTables and PivotCharts drove the visuals.

OUTCOME

Stock was already fair: revenue share and customer share were within 0.3% in every city. The real issues were flat growth (-0.5% in 2023 and +1.6% in 2024 against a 5% target), ₦72.4M in discounts with no clear lift in order value, and two cities that shrank in 2024, Abuja (-5.7%) and Kano (-2.9%).
