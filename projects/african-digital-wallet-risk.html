THE PROBLEM

Investigates fraud and financial risk across 50,000 digital-wallet transactions from 5,000 gig workers in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa -- after discovering the dataset's documentation did not match the data itself, the project became a prove-it-first audit of every hypothesis before any finding was reported.

APPROACH

I built a five-page Excel dashboard on Power Query and Power Pivot with every KPI as a live DAX measure, then tested each fraud hypothesis the brief named formally: chi-square and Cramér's V for all ten categorical dimensions, point-biserial correlation for the continuous ones, and a two-way channel-by-market cut when every one-way test came back flat.

OUTCOME

Ten single-dimension tests all returned negligible Cramér's V (below 0.04), the platform's worker risk score shows zero correlation with actual fraud losses (r = -0.003), and losses are dispersed: the top 10% of workers carry 20.4% of fraud losses, about twice their fair share but far from watchlist territory.
