THE PROBLEM

Telecom retention teams react to churn after it happens. I built a model that ranks 7,043 customers by their probability of leaving, so outreach goes to the right people first, plus an executive dashboard that shows where the risk lives.

APPROACH

I trained a logistic regression on 19 features (DictVectorizer one-hot plus tenure and charges), validated with 5-fold cross-validation on ROC-AUC, pickled the vectorizer and model together, and served real-time predictions through a FastAPI endpoint with Swagger docs.

OUTCOME

Test ROC-AUC 0.858 with cross-validation at 0.842 +/- 0.007, reproduced on re-run. The model separates risk cleanly: month-to-month customers churn at 42.7% versus 2.8% on two-year contracts, and I flagged the 1,869 churned-profile customers (26.5% of the base) whose lifetime billing totals $2.86M.
