THE PROBLEM

Organizations need to know which customers are at risk of churning before they leave, not after, so retention efforts can be targeted rather than reactive.

APPROACH

Built a churn prediction workflow covering data preparation, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, and model training with scikit-learn and XGBoost, served through a FastAPI endpoint.

OUTCOME

Flagged approximately 1,869 at-risk customers from the evaluated dataset for targeted retention follow-up.
