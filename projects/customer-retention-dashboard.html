THE PROBLEM

The churn model can rank every customer, but leadership needs one screen that says who is at risk, how much revenue walks with them, and which segments to act on. I built that screen in Excel on the same 7,043-customer dataset.

APPROACH

I built the analysis in Excel on PivotTables rather than formulas alone: a raw-data sheet, a pivot layer computing every share and total, and a two-level dashboard with slicers for contract type, gender and internet service so every card and chart filters in one click.

OUTCOME

The dashboard reconciles to the row: 1,869 customers at risk, $139,131 of monthly billing and $2.86M of lifetime billing in that segment, 2,173 tech tickets behind them, and the service gaps that mark them: tech support adoption 29%, online security 29%, against 90% phone-service uptake.
